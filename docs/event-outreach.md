## Ready-to-Adapt Talk

**Working title:** From Cookbook Recipe to Soroban Contract: Practical Patterns for Builders

**Abstract:** Building a Soroban contract involves more than translating business logic into Rust. This session walks through practical patterns from the Soroban Cookbook, including authorization, storage and TTL choices, events, testing, and deployment. A runnable example shows how these decisions fit together, where common mistakes arise, and how developers can adapt the pattern to their own project. The session is intended for Rust or blockchain developers who are beginning to build on Stellar.

**Format:** 20-minute talk plus 10 minutes for questions; adapt to the organizer's requirements.

**Demo material:** Select a maintained example from `examples/` and verify it against the current Rust toolchain and Soroban SDK before submitting. Bring a recorded backup in case venue connectivity or live demo setup fails.

## Booth and Workshop Readiness

A booth or hands-on workshop is not confirmed. Before accepting one, assign an owner and confirm staffing, travel, venue requirements, and budget. Prepare and test:

- A one-page project overview linking the [Cookbook](../README.md), [Showcase](../SHOWCASE.md), and contribution guide.
- A short, reproducible Soroban demo with setup instructions and a no-network fallback.
- QR codes to the repository and contribution entry points; test them on mobile.
- A concise explanation of who the Cookbook serves and how contributors can help.
- A lead-capture and follow-up method that collects only information attendees explicitly agree to share.

After the event, record dates, maintainers present, materials used, questions or feedback themes, and follow-up actions. Do not publish attendee names or contact details without permission.
